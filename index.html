About News Publications

Weihan Wang

I lead multimodal pretraining at Z.ai, building the visual foundation of the GLM model family. I trained as a researcher in the Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG) at Tsinghua University.

My current research focuses on native multimodal models, fine-grained perception and understanding, and agents that interact with the real world.
